Record a discovery observation as a self-contained, reviewable folder.

Discovery is the most important part of the project — go deep and produce many figures. Each distinct finding becomes ONE observation: build a focused, professionally-written notebook (meaningful slug, markdown narrative between code cells, several figures saved to its artifacts/), then call `observe` to bundle it.

  observe(
    name="class-imbalance-by-placement",   # kebab-case, meaningful
    notebook_slug="01-target-balance",      # the notebook that produced it
    markdown="## Finding\n...what the figures show, why it matters for modeling...",
    evidence=["balance_by_placement.png", "balance_table.png"]   # >=1 figure from the notebook's artifacts/
  )

This creates `output/discovery/<name>/` containing:
  - observation.md   — your writeup
  - notebook.ipynb   — a snapshot of the notebook that produced it
  - evidence/        — the figures you listed

Requirements (enforced): a meaningful kebab-case name, an existing notebook, and at least one evidence figure — an observation with no visual evidence is rejected. Produce several observations across the dataset (distributions, correlations, target relationships, missingness, segments, leakage checks, …) before leaving discovery.
